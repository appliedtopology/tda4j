### Added

- `PersistentLaplacian(stream, dimension, from, to)`: the persistent Laplacian of a filtered complex, with its matrix,
  eigenvalues, persistent Betti number (the dimension of its kernel) and spectral gap, and the same through
  `persistentLaplacianFromPoints` in the MATLAB/Java interface, `persistentLaplacian` in JavaScript,
  `tda4j.persistent_laplacian` in Python and `--persistent-laplacian` on the command line.
