Plot

org.appliedtopology.tda4j.plot.Plot
See thePlot companion trait
object Plot

The plots of persistent homology. Every function returns a Figure (or a Scene for 3-D) to view(), save(...), or adjust (copy(title = ...), + mark, over).

Homological degree k always has the palette's slot k and its own marker shape, so a degree keeps its color and shape from plot to plot whichever degrees are shown. Anything that takes a diagram also takes a list of bars.

The barcode: one horizontal bar per class from its birth to its death, grouped by degree (lowest on top), each group sorted by birth. An essential bar runs off the right edge, marked ∞. Hover a bar for its numbers.

The barcode: one horizontal bar per class from its birth to its death, grouped by degree (lowest on top), each group sorted by birth. An essential bar runs off the right edge, marked ∞. Hover a bar for its numbers.

Value parameters

def cocycle(points: PointCloud, chain: Terms[Simplex[Int]], at: Optional[Double] = ..., title: String = ...)(using look: Look): Figure

A simplicial cocycle over its point cloud: each edge (or vertex, triangle) with a nonzero value, colored by that value on the palette's diverging pair (negative one way, positive the other; for Z/p, the balanced representative). A 1-cocycle's edges cut across the loop it detects.

A simplicial cocycle over its point cloud: each edge (or vertex, triangle) with a nonzero value, colored by that value on the palette's diverging pair (negative one way, positive the other; for Z/p, the balanced representative). A 1-cocycle's edges cut across the loop it detects.

A bar's cocycle is a cocycle of the complex where the bar dies, so it has long edges right across the data; at restricts it to the cells of diameter at most at (its restriction to the Vietoris-Rips complex there, still a representative for at inside the bar). at = bar.birth shows the few edges that cut the loop as it closes.
