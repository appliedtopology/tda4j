\paragraph{Reproducing the evaluation.}
The harness is in the repository (\code{bench/}). \code{bench/run.sh setup} installs every tool at a pinned version,
\code{prepare} downloads and generates the data (seeded; every download is checked against a recorded checksum), and
\code{bench/final\_run.sh} runs the stages of the final run on a frozen commit and merges them. The raw results (\code{runs.csv},
\code{summary.csv}, the barcodes and \code{env.json}) are archived with the release: \TODO{archive location}. The tables of
this paper are generated from the summaries by \code{papers/tools/bench-to-tex.py}.
